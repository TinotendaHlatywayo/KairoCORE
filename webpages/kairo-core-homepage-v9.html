FAQ

Questions school leaders ask us

Can’t find your answer? Message us on WhatsApp.

Is Kairo CORE built for my type of school?

Yes. It is designed for primary, secondary and tertiary institutions, including boarding schools.

What does it cover?

Admissions, academics and report cards, fees and multi-currency billing, communication, events, clinic, inventory, library, HR and real-time reports.

Is our data safe?

Data is stored in the cloud with advanced security and backups, so records stay protected and available.

Will my team be trained?

Yes. We set up your school, train your staff, and provide video tutorials, a help centre and dedicated support.

Can we access it from any device?

Yes. Kairo CORE is cloud-based, so you can use it from any device, anywhere.
