# Manage student records

The **Students** section is the single source of truth for every enrolled student.

## Finding a student

Open **Students** and use the search box or the table filters:

- Filter by **Form / Grade** to see everyone in a level.
- Search by name, admission number, or student ID number.

## Viewing a record

Click a student row to open their profile. It shows:

- **Personal details** — photo, names, student ID, admission number, gender, date of birth, email, phone.
- **Current enrollment** — academic year, form / grade, and stream / class. These values come from the student's enrolment, so they always reflect the real placement, not stale copies on the student row.

## Editing

Click **Edit** on any record to update contact details, photo, or other fields. To move a student to a different form or stream, edit the enrolment fields (academic year, form, stream) and save.

## Deleting

Deletion is available from the table actions. Only remove records that were created in error — for historical students, keep the record so their fees, attendance and report cards stay linked.
