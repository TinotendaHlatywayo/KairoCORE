# Manage boarding and student welfare

**Boarding & Welfare** is split into **Accommodation** (the physical side) and **Welfare & Care** (the daily duty of care).

## Accommodation

### Hostels

Open **Boarding & Welfare → Accommodation → Hostels**. A hostel record holds its **name**, **type**, **capacity**, **status** and description, plus optional **floors** (number and name) and **wings**. Floors and wings let you describe large boarding houses accurately.

### Rooms

Open **Boarding & Welfare → Accommodation → Rooms**. Each room belongs to a hostel, sits on a floor and wing, and carries:

- **Room number** and **name**
- **Room type** and **capacity**
- **Status**

Keep capacities realistic — allocations respect the bed count.

### Allocations

Open **Boarding & Welfare → Accommodation → Allocations** to place a student:

1. Choose the **student** and **academic year**.
2. Pick the **room** and **bed**.
3. Record the bed number, condition and allocation date.
4. Add an expected checkout date and notes if the stay is temporary.

When a boarder leaves or moves, close the allocation rather than deleting it so the history stays intact.

## Welfare & care

### Out passes

Open **Boarding & Welfare → Welfare & Care → Out Passes** when a boarder needs to leave campus. An out pass records:

- the **student** and **hostel**
- the **type** — Emergency Out-Pass, Weekend Leave, Medical Check or Home Visit
- **expected departure** and **return** times
- the **reason**

Approval is two-step: **Dispatch OTP** sends a one-time code to the parent, then **Verify OTP** with the six-digit code they read back. Only then does the pass show as approved for the scanner checkpoint. This keeps a verifiable record of who authorised the absence.

### Attendance

Open **Boarding & Welfare → Welfare & Care → Attendance** for roll call. Choose the hostel and date, then mark each learner present or absent and add remarks. Boarding attendance is separate from day-school attendance, so both records stay accurate.

### Inspections

Open **Boarding & Welfare → Welfare & Care → Inspections** to record room checks. Inspections score **cleanliness**, **inventory status** and **orderliness**, then mark whether the room **passes inspection**. Notes and the inspector are stored with the date.

> Check the out-pass list and attendance together at lights-out: a learner who is neither in the dorm nor on an approved pass needs immediate follow-up.
