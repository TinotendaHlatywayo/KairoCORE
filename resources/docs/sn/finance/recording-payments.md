# Rekodha mubhadharo uchipesana nemainvoice

Kana mari yasvika (mari kuhofisi, kuendesa kubhengi, mari inotakurwa), inyore uchipesana neinivhoisi yemudzidzi kuitira kuti imwe mari igadziriswe nekukasika.

## Rekodha muripo

1. Vhura **Mari → Kubhadharisa Mudzidzi & Mari yeMari → Mainvoice**.
2. Tsvaga invoice yemudzidzi (tsvaga nezita kana invoice nhamba).
3. Pamutsetse weinvoice, tinya **Rekodha Kubhadhara**.
4. Isa mari, nzira yekubhadhara, zuva, uye referensi (nhamba yerisiti kubva kubhanga rako, EcoCash transaction ID).
5. Sarudza iyo **deposit bank account**, wobva wachengeta.

Iyo invoice **chiyero** inoverengerazve: `total − payments`. Invoice yakabhadharwa zvizere inoratidza zero balance.

### Overpayments

Kana iwe ukapinda kupfuura yasara yasara, iyo sisitimu inomira iyo overpayment mune yekubhadhara dialog kuti iwe ugone kugadzirisa iyo mari kana kuisiya nemaune (semuenzaniso prepayment inoendeswa kune inotevera temu).

## Dhinda risiti

Mushure mekurekodha, tinya **Printa Receipt** pamutsetse mumwe chete kuti upe mubhadhari humbowo hwekubhadhara. Marisiti anotakura yako yakagadziriswa gwaro rekuisa uye cheki chekuchengetedza. **Dhinda Marisiti Akasarudzwa** anodhinda marisiti eboka remainvoice akabhadharwa.

## Mashoko emhuri

Dzvanya **Dhinda Chirevo** kuti uburitse nhoroondo yakazara yekubhadhara yemudzidzi kana mhuri - inobatsira panguva yekuunganidza kana kune vanotsigira.

## Paynow (self-service)

Mitsara ine chiyero inoratidzawo **Bhadhara kuburikidza nePaynow**, iyo inovhura online yekutarisa link - vabereki vanogona kubhadhara online pasina hofisi kutora chero chinhu. Kubhadhara kwepamhepo uye kurodha **Kubhadhara Ufakazi** mirira kusimbiswa kwako zvisati zvakanganisa chiyero.

## Tumira kunze kumaakaundi ako

**Tumira kunze CSV** patafura yemainvoice dhawunirodha runyoro rwakasefa (nhamba dzeinvoice, hwerengedzo, masaramusi) espredishiti kana pasuru yeakaunzi.

## Kugadziridza

Shandisa **Gadzirisa Muripo** painivhoisi kana mutero uchinyatsoda kugadziriswa, nechikonzero chakarekodwa chekuongorora. Usafe wakadzima nhoroondo yekubhadhara.
