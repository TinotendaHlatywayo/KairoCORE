# Tarisa masara emari yemudzidzi

Yese invoice inotakura **yasara** — mari ichiri kufanira mushure mekubhadhara kwese kwakarekodhwa.

## Patafura yemainvoice

Vhura **Mari → Kubhadharisa Mudzidzi & Mari yeMari → Mainvoice**. Mutsara wega wega unoratidza huwandu hweinvoice uye **Balance** (yakaiswa mutsvuku kana mari ichiri kukweretwa). Sefa patafura kuti uwane munhu wese ane chiyero chakasara, kana kutsvaga mhuri imwe.

## Pane dashboard yemari

Vhura **Mari → Dashboard & Analytics → Overview** yemufananidzo wechikoro chose: hwerengedzo inobhadharwa, yakaunganidzwa uye yakatanhamara pamasefa azvino, ane machati emaitiro. **Nheyo dzeMari** muboka rimwechete rinopa maonero ebhuku.

## Kumhuri imwe

1. Vhura inivhoisi yemudzidzi.
2. Dzvanya **Dhinda Chirevo** kuti uwane nhoroondo yakazara yekubhadhara - invoice dzakapihwa, mubhadharo wakagamuchirwa, uye chiyero chekuvhara.

Iri ndiro gwaro rekupa mubereki, mubatsiri kana mumiririri anobvunza kuti "chine chikwereti chei?"

## Kutarisana nezvikwereti

- Shandisa iyo invoice list yakasefa kumainvoice asina kubhadharwa kuita vhiki yekuunganidza.
- Zvinyorwa zvinopa mhuri rekodhi yakajeka, yepamutemo - inoshanda zvakanyanya kupfuura chiyeuchidzo chemuromo.
- Kana mhuri isingakwanisi kubhadhara zvechokwadi, isa **Muripo Waiver** kana shandisa **Kugadzirisa Mari** painivhoisi; usambodzima nhoroondo yekubhadhara.
