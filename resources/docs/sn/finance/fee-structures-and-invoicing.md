# Gadzira zvimiro zvekubhadhara uye budisa ma invoice

Kubhadharira mubhadharo munzvimbo yebasa kune zvikamu zviviri: **zvimiro zvemubhadharo** (izvo fomu yega yega kana giredhi rine chikwereti) uye **ma invoice evadzidzi** (iyo chaiyo bhiri pamudzidzi). Vese vari vaviri vanogara pasi pe **Zvemari → Kubhadharisa Mudzidzi & Revenue**.

## 1. Zvirongwa zvekubhadhara

1. Vhura **Mari → Kubhadharisa Mudzidzi & Mari yeMari → Mamiriro Emubhadharo**.
2. Gadzira chimiro: sarudza gore redzidzo, fomu / giredhi rinoshanda kwariri, uye huwandu.
3. Wedzera zvikamu zvemubhadharo (chidzidzo, mitero, zviitwa) kana ukatenga machaji — **Makamu Emubhadharo** ari muboka rimwe chete.

Zvimiro zvinotyaira injini yekubhadharisa otomatiki uye inotora mubhadharo fomu reinvoice.

## 2. Ipa invoice nemawoko

1. Vhura **Mari → Kubhadharisa Mudzidzi & Mari yeMari → Mainvoice**.
2. Dzvanya **Gadzira**.
3. Sarudza **mudzidzi**, **gore redzidzo** uye **temu**. Fomu rinosefa zvimiro zvemubhadharo zviripo kune izvo zvinoenderana negore remudzidzi uye gore rakasarudzwa.
4. Sarudza zvivako zvekubhadhara kuti ubatanidze. Hwerengedzo inoverengera otomatiki; mubhadharo wekuregererwa (kana wakagadzirirwa) unoshanda semutero.
5. Sevha. Iyo invoice inogadzirwa iine chimiro **Haina kubhadharwa** uye yakasarudzika invoice nhamba.

## 3. Mhanya oto-billing injini

Kune boka rese panguva imwe chete:

1. Patafura yemainvoice, tinya **Mhanya Auto-Kubhadharira Injini**.
2. Sarudza chinangwa chekubhadhara (izvo mafomu / mamakisi uye nguva).
3. Injini inogadzira mainvoice emudzidzi wese anokodzera uyo anga asati atova neinvoice yakavhurika yeiyo scope.

Iyi ndiyo nzira inokurumidza kubhadharisa boka regore pazuva rekutanga retemu.

## 4. Zvinyorwa

Imwe invoice inogona kudhindwa (**Printa Invoice** pamutsetse). Invoice uye masisitimu emarisiti — logo, mavara, nzira dzekubhadhara, callout notes — inodzorwa pasi pe **Mari → Core Accounting & Setup → Zvinyorwa zveMatemplate**.

## 5. Tora mari

Rekodha mari, matransfer uye nharembozha uchipikisana neinvoice, uye printa marisiti — ona [Rekodha kubhadhara](/docs/finance/recording-payments). Mhuri dzinogonawo kubhadhara kuburikidza ne**Bhadhara kuburikidza nePaynow** uye utumire **Payment Proofs** kuti ubvumidzwe.
