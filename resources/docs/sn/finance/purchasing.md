# Kutenga, vatengesi uye stock

Zvinhu zvenyama — zvekushandisa, zvinyorwa, zvekushandisa lab - zvinodzorwa mu **Inventory & Procurement** munzvimbo yebasa yepadivi. Ichi chinyorwa chinobata chikamu chekutenga kubva pamaonero emari; ona [Stock, procurement and fixed assets](/docs/inventory/stock-and-procurement) yegwaro rakazara rekushandisa.

## Suppliers

Vhura **Inventory & Procurement → Procurement → Suppliers** kuchengetedza vatengesi vaunotenga kubva ikozvino: ruzivo rwekusangana, mazwi uye nhoroondo. Marekodhi ekutenga anoramba akabatana nemutengesi akakodzera.

## Procurement

Iro **Procurement** boka rinotevera chikumbiro → kurongeka → kuyerera kwekutumira:

1. **Zvikumbiro Zvekutenga** - vashandi vanosimudza zvavanoda, nezvinhu zvemitsara, huwandu uye mutengo unofungidzirwa. Zvikumbiro zvinotama kubva ku***Draft** kuenda ku**Yakatenderwa**.
2. **Kutenga Odha** - zvikumbiro zvakatenderwa zvinova maodha kune mutengesi, ane zuva reodha, inotarisirwa zuva rekutumira uye nemitengo mitsetse zvinhu.
3. **Zvinhu Zvakagamuchirwa** — nyora izvo chaizvo zvasvika pakupesana neodha yega yega, kusanganisira huwandu hwakagamuchirwa uye hwakarambwa. Kugamuchira zvigadziriso stock otomatiki.

## Stock & inventory

Vhura **Inventory & Procurement → Stock & Inventory** yezvazviri kubata, kupihwa kuvashandi kana madhipatimendi, uye **Stock Adjustments** inorekodha kusvika, nyaya uye kugadzirisa saka kuverenga kunoratidza chokwadi.

## Fixed assets

The **Fixed Assets** boka rinotevera zvinhu zvakakosha-pamusoro (fenicha, makombiyuta, motokari) pamusoro pehupenyu hwavo: rejista yeasset, muchengeti uye nzvimbo, kuderera, uye **Kuchengetedza Asset** zvirongwa. Inobatsira kune inishuwarenzi zvirongwa uye ongororo.

## Mari inoshandiswa maringe nekutenga

- Kushandisa mari zuva nezuva pasina zvinhu zvakabatwa → nyora pasi pe[Mari](/docs/finance/expenses).
- Kutenga zvinhu zvinopinda mustock kana kuva zvinhu → shandisa Inventory & Procurement.
