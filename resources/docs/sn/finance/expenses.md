# Rekodha uye ronga mari yechikoro

Chikamu cheMari chinoteedza mari yese yaunowana kuchikoro chako, zvakasiyana nemari yemudzidzi.

## Kunogara mari

Vhura **Mari → Mari uye Kutenga → Mari inodiwa**. Urwu ndirwo runyorwa rwemari yakanyorwa; iyo **Mari & Kutenga** hub zvakare inokubatanidza iwe nekutenga mafambiro ebasa.

## Categories nemhando

Mari **zvikwata** mabhaketi akafara (Administration, Utilities, Salaries, Maintenance); **mhando** ndiyo chaiyo mitsetse zvinhu mukati meaya mabhakiti (Magetsi, Mvura, Internet, Mudzidzisi mihoro).

Iwe haudi yakaparadzana yekuseta skrini - gadzira ivo uchirekodha mari:

1. Tanga mari itsva.
2. Pa **Mutengo weChikamu** munda, shandisa iyo inline **gadzira** sarudzo uye zita bhaketi.
3. Iyo **Mari Yemhando** munda ndeyekusarudza uye inoshanda nenzira imwechete.

Zvikamu nemhando dzakagadzirwa pano dzinowanikwa pamutengo wega wega wenguva yemberi, saka mazita akanaka anoita kuti mishumo yako inyatso batsira.

## Rekodha mari

1. Vhura **Mari → Mari uye Kutenga → Mari inodiwa** uye tinya **Gadzira**.
2. Bata zita remutengo, chikamu, sarudzo yerudzi, huwandu, account yekubhangi, zuva uye nhamba yereferensi.
3. Wedzera mutengesi / mutengesi ruzivo kana mari yakabhadharwa kune kambani.
4. Sevha.

Mari yacho yava kusanganisirwa muhuwandu hwemari nemishumo.

## Ongorora

Tafura yemitengo inogona kusefa nechikamu, mhando, account yebhangi uye zuva renji - semuenzaniso "zvese zvinoshandiswa muQ3" kana "mihoro yeKurume". **Mari → Dashboard & Analytics → Overview** inopeta mari mumufananidzo wezvemari pamwe chete nekuunganidzwa kwemuripo.

## Kutenga uye stock

Kana uchida kutenga zvinhu nerubatsiro (zvekushandisa, mabhuku eraibhurari, midziyo yemurabhorosi), shandisa chikamu che **Inventory & Procurement** panzvimbo pacho — ona [Purchasing, suppliers and stock](/docs/finance/purchasing).
