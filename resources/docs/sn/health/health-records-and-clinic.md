# Chengetedza marekodhi ehutano hwevadzidzi uye kushanya kukiriniki

Ruzivo rwezvehutano rwakaoma. Vashandi chete vane mvumo yehutano vanogona kuvhura **Health & Safety**, uye marekodhi anofanirwa kubatwa zvinoenderana nemutemo wechikoro chekuchengetedza data.

## Zvinyorwa zvekurapa

Vhura **Hutano & Chengetedzo → Marekodhi ezvehutano → Medical Records**. Mudzidzi wega wega anogona kuve neiyo nhoroondo yekurapa:

- **Boka reropa**
- **Allergies** - iyo imwe chete inonyanya kukosha ndima mune emergency
- **Chronic conditions** — asthma, diabetes, pfari nezvimwe zvakadaro
- **Nhoroondo yejekiseni** — inodzokorora zita rejekiseni nezuva raitwa

Chengetedza allergies uye zvisingaperi mamiriro aripo - vadzidzisi nevabatsiri vekutanga vanovimba nepeji ino kana chimwe chinhu chakashata.

## Kushanya kwekiriniki

Vhura **Utano & Dziviriro → Zvinyorwa Zveutano → Kushanya kwekiriniki** kuti unyore pese paunoshanya kukiriniki yechikoro kana nzvimbo inorwara.

### Bata kushanya

1. Sarudza **mudzidzi** uye rekodha chichemo.
2. Bata zvakakosha kwakatorwa: **tembiricha** uye **BP**.
3. Nyora **zviratidzo**, **kuongororwa** uye **kurapwa kwapihwa**.
4. Seta iyo **status**:
- **Checked In** — ichangosvika
- **Under Active Treatment** — kuchengetwa mukiriniki
- **Kupinzwa muCliniki Ward** — kugara akatariswa
- **Inotumirwa kuGeneral Hospital** - inoda kutarisirwa kunze (nyora **referral destination**)
- **Yakasunungurwa** - kushanya kwakavharwa

### Mishonga

Kunochengeterwa mishonga yechikoro nechikoro, wedzera **zvirevo** pakushanya: sarudza chinhu chiripo (kana nyora zita remushonga), wozorekodha chipimo chemushonga, kuwanda uye huwandu hwakatarwa. Kupa kubva pakushanya kunobatanidza mushonga kune rekodhi yemudzidzi uye kunoderedza uwandu hwemari mukiriniki.

## Tevera

- Sefa kushanya kwekiriniki nechimiro kuti uone kuti ndiani achiri kurapwa kana kuti ari kurapwa.
- Kushanya kwakanzi **Referred** kunofanirwa kunyorwa zita rechipatara kuti vabereki nevashandi vazive kwakaenda mudzidzi.
- Dhinda kana kugovera pfupiso yekushanyira nemubereki pavanotora mwana.

> Usambofa wakarekodha zvehutano mukuzivisa kwemahara-mavara kana mameseji ekutaura - zvichengete murekodhi yekurapa uye kiriniki yekushanyira log panodzorwa kupinda.
