# Seta basa remumba uye mhanyisa zvidzidzo zvepamhepo

Iyo **LMS** module ndipo apo vadzidzisi vanotsikisa basa remakirasi avo uye vadzidzi vanorigamuchira mune yavo portal.

## Tumira basa rechikoro

1. Vhura **LMS →Homework** wobva wadzvanya **Gadzira**.
2. Sarudza iyo **kirasi rukova** uye **musoro**.
3. Ipa basa rakajeka **zita** uye nyora **tsananguro** — zvekuita, kuti ichaongororwa sei, uye chero chinhu chekuunza kukirasi.
4. Seta **zuva rekupedzisira**.
5. Batanidza zvinhu zvinotsigira:
- **Faira** - pepa rekushanda, kuverenga kana template.
- **YouTube link** - chidzidzo chakarekodhwa kana anotsanangura.

Sevha, uye basa rechikoro rinoonekwa kumudzidzi wese ari murukova rwekirasi iyoyo. Iyo tafura inoratidza **yekutumira kuverenga** pachinhu chimwe chete kuti iwe ugone kuona kubatanidzwa nekungotarisa.

## Iyo LMS hub

Vhura **LMS → Homuweki & Zvidzidzo** kuti uone muchidimbu. Hub inounganidza basa remumba rawakaseta uye rinobatanidza zvakananga mune rondedzero - inobatsira pakutanga kwevhiki paunenge uchironga zvekugovera.

## Kudzidzira kwakanaka

- Nyora **zita** sebasa, kwete musoro wenyaya ("Exercise 4.2 — quadratic equations", kwete "Maths").
- Gara uchiisa **zuva rekupedzisira**; basa rapfuura riri nyore kuona mune rondedzero.
- Natanidza pepa rekushanda faira pane kuritsanangura - vadzidzi vanogona kuzoshanda pasina Indaneti.
- Shandisa zvinongedzo zveYouTube zvekudzokorora zvemukati, uye chengeta tsananguro ipfupi zvakakwana kuti uverenge parunhare.

## Ndiani anoona chii

Vadzidzi vanoona basa rechikoro rekirasi yavo yekuyerera uye chidzidzo mune yavo portal yevadzidzi. Vadzidzisi vanoona zvose zvavakaisa; vatariri vechikoro vanogona kuongorora makirasi ese. Kumaka uye kushuma makadhi anogara pasi pe**Mibvunzo & Kudhirowa**, kwete pano — ona [Misa ongororo uye mamaki ekutora](/docs/exams/assessments-and-marks).
