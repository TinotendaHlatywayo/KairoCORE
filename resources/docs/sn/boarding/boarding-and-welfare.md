# Tonga boarding uye kugara zvakanaka kwevadzidzi

**Yekugara & Welfare** yakakamurwa kuita **Pekugara** (rutivi rwemuviri) uye **Welfare & Care** (basa rezuva nezuva rekutarisira).

## Accommodation

### Hostels

Vhura **Kukwira & Welfare → Pekugara → Hostels**. Rekodhi rehositeri rinobata **zita**, **mhando**, **kugona**, **chimiro** uye tsananguro, pamwe nesarudzo **pasi** (nhamba uye zita) uye **mapapiro**. Floor nemapapiro zvinokuita kuti utsanangure dzimba huru dzebhodhingi nemazvo.

### Makamuri

Vhura **Pokugara & Welfare → Pekugara → Makamuri**. Kamuri yega yega ndeye hostel, inogara pasi nebapiro, uye inotakura:

- **Nhamba yekamuri** uye **zita**
- **Imba yemhando** uye **kugona**
- **Status**

Chengetedza hunyanzvi hwechokwadi - migove inoremekedza huwandu hwemubhedha.

### Allocations

Vhura **Kugara & Kugarisana → Pekugara → Kugoverwa** kuisa mudzidzi:

1. Sarudza **mudzidzi** uye **gore redzidzo**.
2. Sarudza **room** ne **mubhedha**.
3. Rekodha nhamba yemubhedha, mamiriro uye zuva rekugovera.
4. Wedzera zuva rinotarisirwa kubuda uye manotsi kana kugara kuri kwechinguvana.

Kana boarder achibva kana kufamba, vhara mugove pane kudzima kuti nhoroondo irambe yakasimba.

## Welfare & care

### Out passs

Vhura **Kugara & Welfare → Welfare & Care → Out Passes** apo mugari anoda kubva pacampus. An out pass anonyora:

- **mudzidzi** uye **hostel**
- the **rudzi** — Emergency Out-Pass, Weekend Leave, Medical Check kana Kushanya Kumba
- **inotarisirwa kuenda** uye **kudzoka** nguva
- **chikonzero**

Mvumo ine nhanho mbiri: **Dispatch OTP** inotumira kodhi yenguva imwe chete kumubereki, ipapo **Simbisa OTP** nekodhi ine manhamba matanhatu yavanodzosera. Ipapo ndipo chete ndipo panoratidza kupasa sekutenderwa kwekutarisa scanner. Izvi zvinochengeta rekodhi inogoneka yekuti ndiani akabvumidza kusavapo.

### Kupinda

Vhura **Kukwira & Welfare → Welfare & Care → Kupinda** pakufona. Sarudza hostel uye zuva, wozomaka mudzidzi wega wega aripo kana asipo uye wowedzera matauriro. Kupinda mubhodhi kwakasiyana nekuenda kuchikoro chemasikati, saka zvinyorwa zvese zvinogara zvakarurama.

### Inspections

Vhura **Kukwira & Welfare → Welfare & Care → Kuongorora** kurekodha cheki dzemakamuri. Zvibodzwa zvekuongorora **kucheneswa**, **mamiriro ezvinyorwa** uye **kurongeka**, wobva wamaka kana kamuri **yapfuura kuongororwa**. Manotsi uye inspector zvinochengetwa nezuva.

> Tarisa rondedzero yekubuda-pasi uye kupinda pamwe chete pamagetsi-kunze: mudzidzi asiri mudorm kana papasi rakabvumidzwa anoda kuteedzerwa nekukurumidza.
