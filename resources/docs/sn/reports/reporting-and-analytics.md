# Vaka mishumo uye ongorora analytics

**Mishumo & Hungwaru** inoshandura iyo data yawakatotora kuita mhinduro. Pane mazinga matatu: nhamba dzedashibhodhi dzinokurumidza, mishumo yetsika yaunovaka kamwe chete uye woshandisazve, uye muongorori wekuongorora ad-hoc.

## Dashboard

Vhura **Mishumo & Hungwaru → Dashboard** yekuongorora kwechikoro: kunyoresa, kuenda, kuunganidzwa kwemubhadharo uye mamwe manhamba emusoro wenguva iripo. Tanga pano mangwanani ega ega kuti uone chero chinhu chinoda kutariswa.

## Reports hub

Vhura **Mishumo & Hungwaru → Mishumo** yehubhu inobatanidza maturusi ari pazasi uye chero akachengetwa mishumo matemplate.

## Report Generator

Vhura **Mishumo & Hungwaru → Mishumo → Report Jenareta**. Gadzira chirevo kubva pakutanga:

1. **Chirevo zita** - uye nesarudzo chengetedza se template inogona kushandiswa zvakare.
2. **Dhata sosi** - imwe kana anopfuura dhataseti (vadzidzi, vashandi, mari, kuenda, zvichingodaro).
3. **Columns** — sarudza minda yekusanganisa. Minda yakanyorwa nekwavo kunobva dataset.
4. **Kujoinha** - paunosarudza zvinopfuura imwe dataset, tsanangura kuti marekodhi anobatana sei.
5. **Mafirita** - nhete kusvika kuboka regore, temu, zuva renji kana chimiro.
6. **Maverengero** - hwerengedzo, mavhareji uye maverengero pandima dzenhamba.
7. **Ronga** - sarudza marongero emitsara.
8. **Chati** — sarudza rudzi rwechati apo pfupiso yekuona inobatsira.
9. **Kubuda kwemaitiro**:
- **PDF gwaro** - yakarongwa uye yakasainwa, yakagadzirira kufaira
- **CSV spreadsheet** — mitsara mbishi yeExcel kana Sheets
- **Excel workbook (.xls)** - yekumwe kugadzirisa
- **JSON data** - yekubatanidza uye dashibhodhi
- **Dhinda maonero** — inovhura dialog yebrowser print dialog
- **Clipboard (HTML)** - mavara akapfuma eemail uye zvinyorwa

Dzvanya **Gadzira** uye mushumo unounganidzwa uye unowedzerwa kuraibhurari yako.

## Generated Reports

Vhura **Mishumo & Hungwaru → Mishumo → Yakagadzirwa Mishumo** kuti uone zvese zvawaburitsa. Mutsara wega wega unoratidza **chimiro** (Yakapedzwa, Kugadziridza kana Kukundikana), **fomati**, **rekodhi kuverenga** uye kugadzirisa nguva.

- **Dhawunirodha** iyo yapera faira.
- **Verify** inotarisa zvakare mushumo uchipokana neiyo live source data - kana data rachinja mushure mekuunganidzwa, mutsetse unokuudza kuti ugadzirise.

## Analytics Explorer

Vhura **Mishumo & Hungwaru → Mishumo → Analytics Explorer** yekuongorora ad-hoc: shandura zviyero uye zviyero zvinopindirana pasina kuvaka chirevo. Ishandise kupindura mibvunzo imwe chete ("ndepi nzizi dzine vanhu vashoma zvakanyanya temu ino?") usati wazvipira kumushumo wakachengetedzwa.

> Sevha rondedzero setemplate kana iwe uchiziva kuti uchaimhanyisa temu yega yega - inotevera nguva yekudzvanya kumwe chete, ine makoramu anowirirana uye mafirita.
