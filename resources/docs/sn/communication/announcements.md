# Tumira zviziviso kuvashandi nemhuri

Zviziviso ndiyo chiteshi chepamutemo chechikoro chezviziviso - kuvharwa, zviitiko, mazuva etemu, zviyeuchidzo zvemubhadharo.

## Gadzira chiziviso

1. Vhura **Nzvimbo Yekutaurirana → Nharaunda & Engagement → Zviziviso**.
2. Nyora **musoro** uye **zviri**. Zvemukati zvinotsigira akapfuma mafomati - misoro, mavara akashinga, zvinyorwa uye zvinongedzo.
3. Sarudza **vateereri**:
- **Inoonekwa Kumabasa** — basa rimwe chete kana anopfuura (vadzidzisi chete, vabereki chete, basa retsika), kana **Mabasa Ese**.
- **Target Specific Individuals** - sarudza vanhu vane mazita ekuzivisa kamwe chete.
4. Sarudza iyo **Delivery Channel**:
- **Sistimu chete** - chiziviso che-mu-app mukati memumwe neumwe portal.
- **Email chete** - inotumira chiziviso chizere neemail.
- **System uye Email** - zvese.
5. Seta iyo **status** - Draft, Yakarongwa, Yakatsikiswa kana Yapera Nguva. `Published` ndiyo yekutanga.
6. Seta **yakaburitswa_pa** kuronga chiziviso, uye **inopera_pa** kana ichizongonyangarika.
7. Sevha.

## Ita kuti zvibude pachena

Zviziviso zvinotakura sarudzo dzemharidzo dzinotonga kuti dzinomhara sei:

- **Chinokosha** — Yakaderera, Yakajairika, Yakakosha, Yakakosha kana Emergency (Emergency yakachengeterwa zviziviso zvekuchengetedza zvechokwadi).
- **Maitirwo ekuratidzira** — Kadhi Rakajairika, Yekuzivisa Banner, Modal Popup kana Scrolling Ticker.
- **Inoda kutenderwa** — vanogamuchira vanofanira kusimbisa kuti vakaiverenga; inobatsira pamatongerwo enyika uye mapfupi ekuchengetedza.
- **Kuwana chekunamirwa** — rega vanogamuchira Vaone & Dhawunirodha zvakanamirwa, kana kuti Ona Chete.

## Kuronga nekuvhara

- A **Yakarongwa** chiziviso chine ramangwana `published_at` inobuda panguva iyoyo.
- Siya `expires_at` isina chinhu kuti chiziviso chirambe chichionekwa nekusingaperi; isa zuva kana kushandura chimiro kuti urege basa.

## Mazano

- Tarisira nechinzvimbo pese paunokwanisa - chiziviso chevabereki veGrade 7 hachidi kuti chipinde mubhokisi remainbox remudzidzisi wese.
- Chengeta mazita ari mapfupi; ndizvo zvinoonekwa nevanhu mumazita ekuzivisa.
- Shandisa **Inoda kutenderwa** kune chero chinhu chaungade kuratidza chakataurwa.
- Pamushandirapamwe uye zviyeuchidzo, funga nezvechiziviso chakazvipira kwete kugadzirisa mainvoice.
