# Gadzira account yako yechikoro wopinda

Chikoro chega chega chinowana chayo yakachengeteka nzvimbo yekushanda uye yayo yeruzhinji webhusaiti. Gwaro iri rinobata matanho ekutanga: kunyoresa, kumisa, uye kusaina.

## 1. Nyoresa chikoro chako

1. Enda kupuratifomu webhusaiti uye tinya **Register** (kana enda ku`/register`).
2. Zadza zita rechikoro, email kero yako, uye password.
3. Tumira fomu racho. Iwe uchaona chisimbiso chekuti account yakagadzirwa uye kuti email yekumisikidza yakatumirwa.

> Kunyoresa kune mwero-inogumira kune chengetedzo. Kana iwe warova muganhu, mira zvishoma uye edza zvakare.

## 2. Activate account yako

1. Vhura iyo activation email uye tinya iyo activation link (kana enda ku`/activate-account` uye isa iyo kodhi kubva kune email).
2. Kana yangoitwa, nzvimbo yako yekushanda yechikoro yakagadzirira.

Kana chinongedzo chapera, shandisa iro peji kukumbira nyowani activation kodhi.

## 3. Saina

1. Enda ku`/login` woisa email yako nepassword.
2. Unomhara pa**Dashboard** yako - mhedziso yezuva rechikoro chako.

Chikwata chako chichashandisa iyo yakafanana nzvimbo yebasa URL (semuenzaniso `your-school.kairo.co.zw/workspace`). Vadzidzi nevabereki havamboshandisi nzvimbo iyi - vanoshandisa webhusaiti yechikoro yeruzhinji uye yavo yekupinda mudzidzi.

## 4. Zvekuita zvinotevera

- **Gadzira chimiro chako chedzidzo** - gore rezvidzidzo, mafomu kana mamakisi, hova, zvidzidzo.
- **Wedzera vadzidzi** - vanyorese zvakananga kana kuongorora zvikumbiro kubva pawebhusaiti yako yeruzhinji.
- **Gadzirisa mari** - zvimiro zvekubhadhara, invoice, uye kubhadhara kurekodha.

Shandisa Categories list iri kuruboshwe kuti uenderere mberi.
