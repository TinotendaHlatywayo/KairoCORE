# Mhanya payroll uye maneja muripo

Payroll inogara pasi pe **HR & Payroll → Payroll & Compensation**. Kuyerera kwacho ndekwekuti: tsanangura mamakisi emuhoro kamwechete, simudza nguva yekubhadhara, rega system iverenge mubhadharo wemunhu mumwe nemumwe, iongorore, wobva wabvumidza kubviswa.

## 1. Salary mamakisi

Vhura **HR & Payroll → Payroll & Compensation → Salary Grade**. A giredhi rinotsanangura mubhadharo wemusana:

- **Zita** uye **base muhoro**
- **Dzimba**, **yekufambisa** uye **yebasa** mvumo
- **Overtime inokodzera**
- **Kubvumidzwa kwetsika** — anodzokorora ane zita, mhando (muzana kana huwandu) uye kukosha
- **Kubviswa kwetsika** - chimiro chakafanana chekubviswa kunodzokororwa

Ipa giredhi kune mumwe nemumwe mushandi pafomu reVashandi. Magiredhi ndiwo anoshandiswa payroll kuita muripo.

## 2. Gadzira nguva yekubhadhara

1. Vhura **HR & Payroll → Kubhadhara & Muripo → Nguva Yekubhadhara**.
2. Dzvanya **New Payroll Period**.
3. Ipe zita (semuenzaniso "Kurume 2026"), isa misi ye **kutanga** uye **yekupedzisira**, uye sarudza **akaundi yekubhangi** iyo mihoro ichabviswa pairi.
4. Sevha.

Iyo nguva inotanga isina chinhu, ine chimiro chaunogona kuona pakungotarisa patafura.

## 3. Verenga uye wedzera mihoro

Pamutsetse wenguva, tinya **Kareta & Populate Mihoro**. Iwe unogona kuderedza kumhanya ne **muhoro giredhi**, **dhipatimendi**, **rudzi rwebasa**, **designation** uye **gender** - inobatsira kana iwe uchibhadhara mapoka evashandi akasiyana pamazuva akasiyana. Iyo sisitimu inogadzira mutsara wekubhadhara kune mumwe nemumwe mushandi anofananidza vachishandisa giredhi ravo, maallowances uye kubviswa.

## 4. Wongorora usati wabvumira

Dzvanya **Tarisa Ledger & Kuputsa** kuti uongorore mitsara yakaverengerwa. Tarisa vashandi vasipo, mamakisi asiri iwo uye kubviswa kusingatarisirwe mari isati yafamba.

## 5. Bvumira uye bvisa

Kana manhamba ari echokwadi, tinya **Bvumira & Deduct Mihoro** uye simbisa **akaundi yekubhangi** yekubvisa kubva. Iyo yakazara inotumirwa kumaakaundi ako uye nguva yakamisikidzwa yakatenderwa.

Kana wabvumira chimwe chinhu nekukanganisa, **Dzorera** inodzosera kumashure kutumira kwenguva (zvichienderana nemvumo dzako) kuti ugone kuigadzirisa uye wotangazve.

> Mhanya mubhadharo chete mushure mekupinda uye kusiya kwave kuenderana - kubviswa uye kusavapo kubata shandisa marekodhi atove muhurongwa.

## Zvikwereti zvevashandi

Vhura **HR & Payroll → Kubhadhara & Muripo → Zvikwereti zveVashandi** kuti uone kufambiswa kwemuhoro uye zvikwereti. Chikwereti chinonyora:

- iyo **mushandi** uye **rudzi rwechikwereti** (Salary Advance, Emergency Loan, Device Loan, Zvimwe)
- iyo **bank account** inobhadharwa kubva
- iyo **yakawanda mari**, nzira yekubhadhara, chiyero chemubereko uye rudzi rwemhindu
- iyo **yakazara inobhadharwa**, **chiyero chasara** uye kubviswa kwepamwedzi (yakagadziriswa huwandu kana muzana yehuwandu)

Payroll inobvisa mari yechikwereti kubva pamubhadharo wemushandi kusvika mari yapera, saka chengetedza yasara yakaringana kana wagamuchira ad-hoc mubhadharo.
