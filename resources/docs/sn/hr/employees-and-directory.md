# Wedzera uye maneja zvinyorwa zvevashandi

**HR & Payroll → Staff Directory & HR** ndiyo imba yevashandi vese. The **Vashandi** tab inobata rekodhi yakazara yemunhu wega wega; **Nzvimbo yeVashandi** uye **Makasi Ekuranga** muboka rimwechete rambai makabatana nerekodhi iyoyo.

## Wedzera mumwe mushandi

1. Vhura **HR & Payroll → Staff Directory & HR → Vashandi**.
2. Dzvanya **Mushandi Mutsva**, wobva washanda kuburikidza ne wizard:
- **Ruzivo rwemunhu** — mazita, chitupa chenyika, zuva rekuzvarwa, murume kana mukadzi, mamiriro ewanano, nharembozha uye kero.
- **Basa** - dhipatimendi, kusarudzwa, basa, rudzi rwebasa, zuva rakabatanidzwa, zuva rekupedzisira rekontrakiti (yevashandi vekondirakiti) uye chimiro.
- **Muhoro** - giredhi remuhoro, pamwe nechero miripo yakanangana nemunhu uyu.
- **Mhuri & hama yepedyo** - ruzivo rwemukwanyina, vanovimba navo uye vekukurumidzira vekubata.
- **Manotsi ehutano & ezvokurapa** - mamiriro, allergies uye chero chinhu chinofarirwa nemunhu wekutanga.
- **Minda yetsika** — chero mamwe mapeya ekuwedzera zvaunoda kuchikoro (semuenzaniso "TSC nhamba").
3. Sevha.

Kuchengetedza mushandi zvinopa yakabatana portal account otomatiki. Iye munhu anogamuchira activation email kekutanga account yagadzirwa; kupinza kana kuchengetedza zvakare hakutumire zvakare.

## Kugadzirisa dhairekitori

- **Tsvaga uye kusefa** tafura nezita, dhipatimendi, basa, rudzi rwebasa kana chimiro.
- **Shandura Status** pamutsara inofambisa munhu pakati peAnoshanda, Akamiswa, Pakuenda uye Akamiswa. Kudzima munhu anochengeta nhoroondo yavo - usadzima vashandi vane payroll, kusiya kana marekodhi emidziyo.
- **Kurudzira** inorekodha kukwidziridzwa negiredhi idzva uye zuva rekushanda.
- **Govera Asset** inopa chimwe chinhu kubva kurejista-yemidziyo kune mushandi; inoonekwa pasi pe **Staff Assets**.
- Shandisa zviito zvakawanda ku**kutumira maemail ekuvhura** kana kushandura chimiro chevanhu vakati wandei kamwechete.

## Tumira mazita evashandi vese

Kuwedzera vanhu vakawanda nemaoko kunononoka. Shandisa chinotenga kunze spreadsheet panzvimbo — ona [Import staff kubva paspredishiti](/docs/hr/importing-staff-from-spreadsheet).

## Zvinhu zvevashandi uye nyaya dzekuranga

- **Nzvimbo yeVashandi** inoratidza zvakabatwa nemushandi wega wega - malaptop, makiyi, mota - nezuva rakapihwa. Dzosera asset kana munhu aenda.
- **Mamiriro Ekuita** anorekodha zviitiko, vashandi vanobatanidzwa, mhedzisiro uye chero mazuva ekutevera. Zvinoonekwa chete kune vanhu vane HR kuwana.
