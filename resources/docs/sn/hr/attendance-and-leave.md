# Tarisa vashandi uye woenda

Maturusi ese ari maviri anogara pasi pe **HR & Payroll → Kupinda & Kusiya**. Kuzvichengeta zvazvino kunoita kuti kubviswa kwemubhadharo uye kusavapo kwekutaura kuve kwakavimbika.

## Kupinda kwevashandi

Vhura **HR & Payroll → Kupinda & Kubuda → Kupinda Kwevashandi**.

1. Dzvanya **Gadzira**.
2. Sarudza **mushandi**, iyo **zuva**, uye **chimiro**: Iripo, Asipo, Kunonoka, Hafu Zuva kana Kuregererwa.
3. Rekodha **cheki-mukati** uye **tarisa-kunze** nguva kana chikoro chako chichitevera maawa.
4. Sarudza **nzira**: Manual Ledger, RFID Card Scan, Biometric Fingerprint kana QR Kiosk Check-In.
5. Sevha.

Tafura inogona kusefa nemushandi, zuva renji, chimiro uye nzira - inobatsira kupfupiso yemwedzi nemwedzi uye yekuona kusavapo kusati kwatanga kubhadharwa.

> Kana chikoro chako chichishandisa RFID, biometric kana QR zvishandiso, zvinyorwa zvinouya zvoga nenzira yekufananidza. Shandisa Manual Ledger chete kugadzirisa uye kurekodha kamwe chete.

## Siya zvikumbiro

Vhura **HR & Payroll → Kupinda & Siya → Siya Zvikumbiro**.

### Tsanangura mhando dzekusiya paunenge uchienda

Iyo **Kusiya Type** ndima ine inline yekugadzira sarudzo. Mhando yekusiya inotakura:

- **Zita** — Kuenda Kwegore, Kuenda Kurwara, Kuenda Kunosunungukira, zvichingodaro
- **Maximum mazuva pagore**
- **Tsanangudzo** yevashandi

Mhando dzakagadzirwa dzinowanikwa pane zvese zvikumbiro zvenguva yemberi.

### Bata chikumbiro

1. Dzvanya **Gadzira**.
2. Sarudza **mushandi** uye **siya mhando**.
3. Seta iyo **yekutanga** uye **yekupedzisira** misi uye nyora **chikonzero**.
4. Sevha. Zvikumbiro zvitsva zvinotanga se**Pending**.

### Bvuma kana kuramba

Pamutsetse wechikumbiro wakamirira, shandisa **Bvumira** kana **Ramba**. Chikumbiro bheji rinoratidza mhedzisiro (Yakamirira, Yakatenderwa, Yakarambwa), uye mazuva anobva abatwa sekusipo kwemvumo.

## Kudzidzira kwakanaka

- Bvumira zororo nguva yekubhadhara isati yavhara kuitira kuti kubviswa kwekusavapo kugare kwakarurama.
- Chengetedza **akanyanya mazuva pagore** echokwadi pamhando yezororo - ndiyo inoshandiswa nevashandi pakukumbira.
- Shandisa iyo sefa yekudzima zvikumbiro zvakamirira muvhiki nevhiki HR wongororo.
