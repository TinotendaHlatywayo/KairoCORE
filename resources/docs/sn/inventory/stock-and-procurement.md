# Stock, procurement uye fixed assets

**Inventory & Procurement** inovhara zvese zvazvinazvo uye zvinotengwa nechikoro: zvitoro zvezuva nezuva, mapepa ekutenga, uye rejista yeasset.

## Stock & inventory

### Zvinhu uye nzvimbo

Vhura **Inventory & Procurement → Stock & Inventory → Inventory Zvinhu**. Chinhu chimwe nechimwe chine:

- **Zita**, **SKU**, **bhakodhi** uye **chikamu** (mapoka anogona kugadzirwa mukati)
- **Chinhu chemhando** uye **chikamu chechiyero** (zvidimbu, mabhokisi, marita)
- **Huwandu hwazvino** uye **avhareji mutengo weyuniti**
- **Reorder level** - nzvimbo iyo chinhu chinoda kudzokororwa
- **Zuva rakagamuchirwa**, **inotengeswa** mureza uye **mutengo wekutengesa** uko chikoro chinotengesa zvinhu

Zvinhu zvinochengeterwa ku**nzvimbo**, saka chinhu chimwe chete chinogona kuwanikwa mudura nemurabhoritari.

### Kuburitsa stock

Vhura **Inventory & Procurement → Stock & Inventory → Kubudiswa** kana chimwe chinhu chabva muchitoro. Sarudza chinhu, nzvimbo uye batch, isa huwandu, uye rekodha kuti ndiani akazvigamuchira (vashandi, dhipatimendi kana mudzidzi). Zvinhu zvinodzoreka zvinogona kutakura zuva rekudzoka rinotarisirwa uye mamiriro.

### Kugadziriswa kwemasheya

Vhura **Inventory & Procurement → Stock & Inventory → Magadzirirwo eStock** yekuverenga kana kugadzirisa. Kugadziriswa kunofananidza iyo **system uwandu** ne **huwandu hwemuviri** yechinhu chimwe nechimwe, inoverengera musiyano, uye inobvunza chikonzero. Iyi ndiyo nzira yakatendeseka yekugadzirisa kuverenga - usafe wakagadzirisa huwandu zvakananga.

## Procurement

The **Procurement** boka rinotevera cheni yakajeka:

### 1. Zvikumbiro zvekutenga

**Procurement Requests** regai vashandi vabvunze zvavanoda. Chikumbiro chine nhamba yekukumbira, mukumbi, kukurumidza, dhipatimendi, chinangwa, uye mutsara zvinhu zvine huwandu, inofungidzirwa mutengo weyuniti uye zvakatemwa. Inobatawo mazita emukumbiri uye emukuru / masiginicha.

Dzvanya **Bvumira** pachikumbiro chakamirira, sarudza (kana gadzira) iyo **inodiwa mutengesi**, uye sisitimu inogona kufamba yakananga kuodha.

### 2. Kutenga mirairo

**Purchase Orders** ndizvo zvaunotumira kune mutengesi: nhamba yekuraira, mutengesi, zuva rekurongeka, zuva rinotarisirwa kuendesa uye mutengo wemutsara zvinhu. Shandisa **Prinda** kugadzira PDF yemutengesi.

Kana odha yasimbiswa, tinya **Bvumira** uye sarudza iyo yekubhangi account yekubvisa kubva. Zvinhu zvave kutarisirwa zvinopesana nehurongwa uhu.

### 3. Zvinhu zvakagamuchirwa

Kana kutumirwa kwasvika, vhura **Zvinhu Zvakagamuchirwa** uye zvirekore uchipikisana nekuraira kwekutenga. Pamutsara wega wega unogona kutora:

- huwandu hwakaodha, hwakambogamuchirwa uye hwasara
- **huwandu hunogamuchirwa** uye **huwandu hwakarambwa**
- batch nhamba uye zuva rekupera

Shandisa **Enzanisa GRN** pane yekutenga odha kuti uone kuti kutumirwa kwakamira sei. Huwandu hwakatambirwa anogadziridza stock otomatiki, kusanganisira mutengo weavhareji.

## Suppliers

**Vatengesi** inobata vatengesi vako: zita, munhu wekubata, foni, email, kero, mutero / VAT nhamba uye yekuvhura chiyero. Iwe unogona kugadzira mutengesi inline uchibvumira chikumbiro chekutenga.

## Fixed assets

Zvinhu zvepamusoro-soro zvinotevedzwa zvakasiyana pasi pe **Fixed Assets** boka:

- **Fixed Assets** - nhamba yeasset, zita, serial, mutengo wekutenga, salvage kukosha, kukosha kwezvino, hupenyu hunobatsira uye nzira yekuderera. Zvinhu zvinogona kupihwa muchengeti uye nzvimbo.
- **Kugadziriswa kweAsset** - masheti ekugadzirisa uye nhoroondo ine nyika dzakamirira / dzapfuura.

Iri rejista rinodyisa zvirongwa zveinishuwarenzi, maodhisheni uye mabasa emidziyo yevashandi.

> Reorder mazinga anongobatsira kana ukaachengeta. Zviise pachinhu chimwe uye tarisa rondedzero usati watenga mwedzi-kupera.
