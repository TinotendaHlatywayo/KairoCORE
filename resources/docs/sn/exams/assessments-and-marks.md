# Gadzira maassessment uye maki ekutora

Zvese zvakaiswa mumagiredhi muchikoro - bvunzo dzekirasi, mabasa, epakati-temu, fainari - inoyerera nemu**Mibvunzo & Kudhidha**.

## 1. Gadzira kuongorora

1. Vhura **Mibvunzo & Giredhi → Yekuongorora Center → Digital Ongororo**.
2. Dzvanya **Gadzira** uye ipa ongororo**zita** (semuenzaniso "Form 2 Mid-Term Maths").
3. Sarudza **nyaya**, **kirasi rukova**, **gore redzidzo**, **temu** uye **yekuongorora mhando**.
4. Seta iyo **mamaki ese** uye **maki yekupasa**, uye sarudza kuti **inopa mugiredhi here**.
5. Optionally wedzera mirairo, nguva, yekuedza miganhu uye mubvunzo randomisation.
6. Sevha.

## 2. Capture mamaki

1. Vhura **Mibvunzo & Kudhidha → Kudhirowa & Kutungamira kweMakasi → Maki Entry**.
2. Isa mamakisi zvakananga mutafura, kana shandisa iyo **kuzadza** chiitiko kurodha mudzidzi wese mukirasi uye chidzidzo kamwechete.
3. Sevha sezvaunoenda - mamaki anogara achigadzirika kudzamara mushumo wavanodyisa waburitswa.

Digital ongororo dzakagadzirwa kuburikidza nepuratifomu dzinogona zvakare kudyisa mamaki mukati pasina kutaipa zvakare.

## 3. Wongorora usati watsikisa

Shandisa **Mibvunzo & Kudhidha → Kudhirowa & Kutarisira Mako → Performance Analytics** kuti utarise maavhareji ekirasi uye ekunze usati wavharira chero chinhu mukati. **Zvikero zvekuGiredhi** muboka rimwe chete tsanangurai kuti mamaki mepu kusvika kumagiredhi nemacomments sei.

## 4. Kubva mamakisi kusvika kumakadhi eshumo

Kana mamakisi apinda, gadzira zvinyorwa zvedzidzo - ona [Gadzira makadhi emishumo](/docs/exams/report-cards).
