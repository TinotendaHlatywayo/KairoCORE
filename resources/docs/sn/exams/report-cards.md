# Gadzira uye buritsa makadhi emishumo

Makadhi erepoti anovakwa kubva pane mamakisi awatora, akapfekedzwa nekutaura kwechikoro chako, obva aburitswa nekudhindwa. Zvese zvinogara pasi pe**Mibvunzo & Giredhi → Mishumo & Yedzidzo Publishing**.

## 1. Gadzira mishumo

1. Vhura **Mibvunzo & Giredhi → Mishumo & Dzidzo Publishing → Mishumo Makadhi**.
2. Shandisa **Report Matemplate** muboka rimwechete kutanga kana uchida marongerwo echivanhu.
3. Dzvanya **Gadzira Mishumo**, sarudza **gore redzidzo**, **temu** uye **makirasi erukova**, uye template yekushandisa. Iyo sisitimu inovaka mushumo mumwechete pamudzidzi kubva kune avo akabatwa mamaki.

## 2. Wedzera mashoko

- **Mashoko eMudzidzisi weKirasi** uye **Mashoko eMusoro wechikoro** anorekodhwa pamushumo wega wega. Shandisa **Wedzera Kirasi Marekisheni eMudzidzisi** kuti uise ongororo imwe chete parukova rwekirasi yese.
- **Pa-chidzidzo chekutaura** batanidza chirevo kune yega yega mutsara wezvidzidzo.
- **Zvakabudirira Zvakanakisa** uye chiyero chehunyanzvi chinogona kurekodhwa pamudzidzi wega wega uko chikoro chako chinozvishandisa.

## 3. Shambadzira

Mishumo inotanga se **Draft**. Ongorora mamaki nezvataurwa, wobva wadzvanya **Publish to Portal** pamutsara wega wega — kana sarudza akati wandei woshambadza zvakawanda. Mishumo yakadhindwa ndiyo inoonekwa nemhuri uye zvinoshandiswa pakudhinda.

Unoda kugadzirisa chimwe chinhu gare gare? **Kusaburitswa kubva kuPortal** kunovhurazve mushumo wekugadzirisa.

## 4. Dhinda

- **Printa Report** pamutsetse mumwe chete anodhinda kadhi remudzidzi mumwe chete.
- **Mishumo Yakawanda Yakadhinda** inodhinda zvese zvinoenderana nemasefa etafura yako - sePDF rakasanganiswa (rakanakira kirasi yese) kana seZIP yemaPDF ega ega.
- **Dhinda Zvasarudzwa** anodhinda chete mitsetse yamaka.

> Gara uchitarisa-tarisa rondedzero imwe isati yadhindwa yakawanda - kuseta izwi risiri iro rakachipa kubata papepa rimwe pane mazana maviri.

Zvinotevera: **Shambadzira kune Mudzidzi Portal** muboka rimwe chete inodzora izvo mhuri dzinoonekwa mune yavo portal.
