# Run payroll and manage compensation

Payroll lives under **HR & Payroll → Payroll & Compensation**. The flow is: define salary grades once, raise a payroll period, let the system calculate each person's pay, review it, then approve the deduction.

## 1. Salary grades

Open **HR & Payroll → Payroll & Compensation → Salary Grades**. A grade defines the pay spine:

- **Name** and **base salary**
- **Housing**, **transport** and **duty** allowances
- **Overtime eligible**
- **Custom allowances** — a repeater with a name, type (percentage or amount) and value
- **Custom deductions** — the same shape for recurring deductions

Assign a grade to each employee on the Employees form. Grades are what payroll uses to work out pay.

## 2. Create a payroll period

1. Open **HR & Payroll → Payroll & Compensation → Payroll Periods**.
2. Click **New Payroll Period**.
3. Give it a name (for example "March 2026"), set the **start** and **end** dates, and choose the **bank account** the salaries will be deducted from.
4. Save.

The period starts empty, with a status you can see at a glance in the table.

## 3. Calculate and populate salaries

On the period's row, click **Calculate & Populate Salaries**. You can narrow the run by **salary grade**, **department**, **employment type**, **designation** and **gender** — useful when you pay different staff groups on different dates. The system creates a payroll line for each matching employee using their grade, allowances and deductions.

## 4. Review before approving

Click **View Ledger & Breakdown** to inspect the calculated lines. Check for missing employees, wrong grades and unexpected deductions before money moves.

## 5. Approve and deduct

When the figures are right, click **Approve & Deduct Salaries** and confirm the **bank account** to deduct from. The totals post to your accounts and the period is marked approved.

If you approved something by mistake, **Undo** reverses the period's posting (subject to your permissions) so you can correct it and re-run.

> Run payroll only after attendance and leave are up to date — deductions and absence handling use the records already in the system.

## Staff loans

Open **HR & Payroll → Payroll & Compensation → Staff Loans** to track salary advances and loans. A loan records:

- the **employee** and **loan type** (Salary Advance, Emergency Loan, Device Loan, Other)
- the **bank account** it is paid from
- the **principal amount**, repayment method, interest rate and interest type
- the **total repayable**, **balance remaining** and the monthly deduction (fixed amount or percentage of the total)

Payroll deducts the loan instalment from the employee's pay until the balance clears, so keep the remaining balance accurate when you receive ad-hoc repayments.
