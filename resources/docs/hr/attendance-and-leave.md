# Track staff attendance and leave

Both tools live under **HR & Payroll → Attendance & Leave**. Keeping them current makes payroll deductions and absence reporting trustworthy.

## Staff attendance

Open **HR & Payroll → Attendance & Leave → Staff Attendance**.

1. Click **Create**.
2. Choose the **employee**, the **date**, and the **status**: Present, Absent, Late, Half Day or Excused.
3. Record **check-in** and **check-out** times if your school tracks hours.
4. Choose the **method**: Manual Ledger, RFID Card Scan, Biometric Fingerprint or QR Kiosk Check-In.
5. Save.

The table can be filtered by employee, date range, status and method — useful for monthly summaries and for verifying absence before payroll runs.

> If your school uses RFID, biometric or QR devices, entries arrive automatically with the matching method. Use Manual Ledger only for corrections and one-off records.

## Leave requests

Open **HR & Payroll → Attendance & Leave → Leave Requests**.

### Define leave types as you go

The **Leave Type** field has an inline create option. A leave type carries:

- **Name** — Annual Leave, Sick Leave, Maternity Leave, and so on
- **Maximum days per year**
- **Description** for staff

Created types become available on every future request.

### Capture a request

1. Click **Create**.
2. Choose the **employee** and **leave type**.
3. Set the **start** and **end** dates and write the **reason**.
4. Save. New requests start as **Pending**.

### Approve or reject

On a pending request's row, use **Approve** or **Reject**. The request's badge shows the outcome (Pending, Approved, Rejected), and the dates are then treated as authorised absence.

## Good practice

- Approve leave before the payroll period closes so absence deductions stay correct.
- Keep **maximum days per year** realistic per leave type — it is the reference staff use when requesting.
- Use the status filter to clear pending requests in a weekly HR review.
