# Add and manage staff records

**HR & Payroll → Staff Directory & HR** is the home of every staff member. The **Employees** tab holds the full record for each person; **Staff Assets** and **Disciplinary Cases** in the same group stay linked to that record.

## Add one employee

1. Open **HR & Payroll → Staff Directory & HR → Employees**.
2. Click **New Employee**, then work through the wizard:
   - **Personal information** — names, national ID, date of birth, gender, marital status, contact details and address.
   - **Employment** — department, designation, role, employment type, date joined, contract end date (for contract staff) and status.
   - **Salary** — the salary grade, plus any individual allowances specific to this person.
   - **Family & next of kin** — spouse details, dependents and emergency contacts.
   - **Health & medical notes** — conditions, allergies and anything a first-aider should know.
   - **Custom fields** — any extra pairs your school needs (for example "TSC number").
3. Save.

Saving the employee provisions a linked portal account automatically. The person receives an activation email the first time an account is created; re-importing or re-saving does not re-send it.

## Managing the directory

- **Search and filter** the table by name, department, role, employment type or status.
- **Change Status** on a row moves someone between Active, Suspended, On Leave and Terminated. Deactivating a person keeps their history — do not delete staff who have payroll, leave or asset records.
- **Promote** records a promotion with the new grade and effective date.
- **Assign Asset** hands an item from the fixed-asset register to the employee; it appears under **Staff Assets**.
- Use bulk actions to **send activation emails** or change the status of several people at once.

## Import a whole staff list

Adding dozens of people by hand is slow. Use the spreadsheet importer instead — see [Import staff from a spreadsheet](/docs/hr/importing-staff-from-spreadsheet).

## Staff assets and disciplinary cases

- **Staff Assets** shows what each employee holds — laptops, keys, vehicles — with the date issued. Return the asset when the person leaves.
- **Disciplinary Cases** records incidents, the staff involved, the outcome and any follow-up dates. It is visible only to people with HR access.
