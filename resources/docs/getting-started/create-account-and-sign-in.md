# Create your school account and sign in

Every school gets its own secure workspace and its own public website. This guide covers the very first steps: registering, activating, and signing in.

## 1. Register your school

1. Go to the platform website and click **Register** (or go to `/register`).
2. Fill in the school name, your email address, and a password.
3. Submit the form. You will see a confirmation that the account was created and that an activation email was sent.

> Registration is rate-limited for security. If you hit a limit, wait a few minutes and try again.

## 2. Activate your account

1. Open the activation email and click the activation link (or go to `/activate-account` and enter the code from the email).
2. Once activated, your school workspace is ready.

If the link has expired, use the same page to request a fresh activation code.

## 3. Sign in

1. Go to `/login` and enter your email and password.
2. You land on your **Dashboard** — the overview of your school's day.

Your team will use the same workspace URL (for example `your-school.kairo.co.zw/workspace`). Students and parents never use this area — they use the school's public website and their own student login.

## 4. What to do next

- **Set up your academic structure** — academic year, forms or grades, streams, subjects.
- **Add students** — enrol them directly or review applications from your public website.
- **Configure finance** — fee structures, invoicing, and payment recording.
