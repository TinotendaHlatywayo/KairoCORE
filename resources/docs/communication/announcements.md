# Send announcements to staff and families

Announcements are the school's official channel for notices — closures, events, term dates, fee reminders.

## Create a notice

1. Open **Communication Center → Community & Engagement → Announcements**.
2. Write the **title** and **content**. Content supports rich formatting — headings, bold text, lists and links.
3. Choose the **audience**:
   - **Visible to Roles** — one or more roles (teachers only, parents only, a custom role), or **All Roles**.
   - **Target Specific Individuals** — pick named people for one-off notices.
4. Choose the **Delivery Channel**:
   - **System only** — an in-app notification inside each recipient's portal.
   - **Email only** — sends the full notice by email.
   - **System and Email** — both.
5. Set the **status** — Draft, Scheduled, Published or Expired. `Published` is the default.
6. Set **published_at** to schedule the notice, and **expires_at** if it should disappear automatically.
7. Save.

## Make it stand out

Announcements carry presentation options that control how loudly they land:

- **Priority** — Low, Normal, Important, Critical or Emergency (Emergency is reserved for genuine safety notices).
- **Display style** — Standard Card, Alert Banner, Modal Popup or Scrolling Ticker.
- **Requires acknowledgement** — recipients must confirm they've read it; useful for policies and safety briefings.
- **Attachment access** — let recipients View & Download attachments, or View Only.

## Scheduling and closing

- A **Scheduled** notice with a future `published_at` goes out at that time.
- Leave `expires_at` empty to keep the notice visible indefinitely; set a date or change the status to retire it.

## Tips

- Target by role whenever you can — a Grade 7 parents notice doesn't need to land in every teacher's inbox.
- Keep titles short; they're what people see in notification lists.
- Use **Requires acknowledgement** for anything you may need to prove was communicated.
- For fee campaigns and reminders, consider a dedicated announcement rather than editing invoices.
