# Send announcements to staff and families

Announcements are the school's official channel for notices — closures, events, term dates, fee reminders.

## Create a notice

1. Open **Communication Center → Notice / Announcement**.
2. Write the title and body.
3. Choose the **audience**: all users, specific roles (teachers only, parents only, a custom role), or specific individuals.
4. Choose the **delivery**:
   - **In-app** — appears inside each recipient's portal.
   - **Email** — sends the full notice to matching email addresses.
   - **Email only** — skips the in-app copy.
5. Set **status** to Published, and optionally a **published_at** date/time to schedule it.
6. Save.

A published notice with email delivery triggers the announcement email immediately (or at the scheduled time).

## Scheduling and closing

- Set **published_at** in the future to queue a notice — for example Friday afternoon for Monday morning.
- Leave the expiry empty to keep the notice visible indefinitely; set a date or change the status to retire it.

## Tips

- Target by role whenever you can — a Grade 7 parents notice doesn't need to land in every teacher's inbox.
- Keep titles short; they're what people see in notification lists.
- For fee campaigns and reminders, consider a dedicated announcement rather than editing invoices.
