# Build and publish your school website

Every school on the platform gets a public website. The **Website** module is the content management system behind it — no coding required.

## 1. Templates & design

Open **Website → Templates & Design → Templates & Design** (the hub). From here you can:

- **create**, **duplicate** and **delete** named site templates
- set the **active template** for your live site
- open the **Design Studio** for advanced design work
- open the **Content Manager** to edit what the site says

The **Websites** tab holds the site record itself:

- **Template gallery** / **active template**
- **Font** and **primary/secondary colours**
- **SEO title suffix** — appended to page titles for search engines

Choose a template that matches your school's character, then adjust the colours to your brand.

## 2. Pages

Open **Website → Templates & Design → Pages**. Each page has:

- **Title** and **slug** — the slug is the page's web address, so keep it short and stable
- **Published** toggle — unpublished pages are hidden from the public site
- **Homepage** toggle — exactly one page should be the homepage
- **Website** — which site the page belongs to

Use **Visual Editor** on a page to arrange its content, or edit the content through the Content Manager.

## 3. Content

Open **Website → Templates & Design → Content Manager**. It edits the **content blocks** inside the active template — headings, text, images, call-to-action labels and card collections — while the design stays locked. That means staff can update the message without breaking the layout.

Upload images directly from the tool where a block expects one.

## 4. Publish

1. Make sure the pages you want are **Published**.
2. Confirm the homepage is marked.
3. Check the site in a browser — use the preview where available, and test on a phone.

Your admissions page (`/apply-online`), contact details and announcements all live on this site. Families arriving from search or social media land here first, so keep term dates and contact information current.

> Change the **slug** of a live page only when you must — old links will stop working. Prefer editing the title and content.
