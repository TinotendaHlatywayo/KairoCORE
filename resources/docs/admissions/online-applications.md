# Receive and process online applications

Every school site built on the platform includes a public **Apply Online** page. Families submit applications there; your staff review them in the workspace.

## 1. The public application page

On your school's website, the admissions page lives at `/apply-online`. Applicants fill in their details and upload the documents you require, then submit.

You can customise the admission form and required documents under **Admissions → Admission Settings** in the workspace.

## 2. Review incoming applications

1. Open **Admissions → Applications** in the workspace sidebar.
2. New applications appear in the list with their status.
3. Open an application to see the full submission and uploaded documents.

## 3. Admit the student

From the application you can admit the candidate. Admission creates the student record — the student then appears under **Students** with the academic year, form and stream carried over from the application where applicable.

## 4. Invoice the fees

After admission, switch to **Finance → Student Billing & Revenue → Invoices** and create the invoice (individually or with the auto-billing engine — see [Invoicing](/docs/finance/fee-structures-and-invoicing)).

## Application statuses you will see

Applications move through submitted, under review and admitted (and rejected where relevant). Always open the record rather than relying on the list colour alone — the full detail view shows everything that was submitted.
