# System settings and subscription

Two areas control the workspace itself: **System Administration** for how it looks and behaves, and **Subscription & Billing** for your plan.

## System settings

Open **System Administration → System Settings**. The settings are grouped into sections:

### Branding

- **Theme & typography** — pick a branding theme, choose whether it applies to the whole workspace or the sign-in pages, and set the font (with a live preview)
- **School logo** — upload the logo and favicon, set the logo height and opacity
- **Dashboard watermark** — upload a background image and control its opacity and scaling

Changes apply to the workspace, printable documents and the portal, so upload a high-resolution logo.

### Module switches

The modules that appear in the sidebar can be toggled on or off — for example **Admissions & Online CRM**, **Students Directory**, **Academics & Curriculum**, finance, HR, inventory, library, boarding, health, communication, LMS, website, reports and administration. Turning a module off hides it and its menus for your school without deleting any data.

Use this to keep the workspace focused: a day school with no boarding doesn't need the Boarding menus.

> Module switches change navigation only. Existing records remain intact and reappear if you switch the module back on.

## Email configuration

Open **System Administration → System Settings → Email Configuration** to set the sender name and address used for activation emails, announcements and password resets. Test delivery after any change.

## User management and audit

- **System Administration → User Management** — accounts, roles and departments. See [Manage staff accounts and permissions](/docs/administration/staff-permissions).
- **System Administration → Audit & Compliance → Audit Log** — who changed what, and when.
- **System Administration → Audit & Compliance → Data Export** — export your school's data.

## Subscription & billing

Open **Subscription & Billing → Subscription & Billing → Overview & Billing** to see:

- your current plan and how many **days remain**
- **credit balance** — lump-sum advance payments, deducted automatically on renewal dates
- the next payment date (or "Trial Active" while on trial)
- **invoices**, with paid/unpaid status and downloadable PDFs

Renew through **Paynow** (EcoCash, OneMoney, ZimSwitch cards, Visa/Mastercard) or record a manual bank deposit transfer. **My Subscription** shows the plan name, status and renewal expiration date, with a link to the billing portal.

> Renew before the expiration date to avoid interruption for staff and families. On a trial, check the days-remaining counter on the dashboard.
