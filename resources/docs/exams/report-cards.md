# Produce and publish report cards

Report cards are built from the marks you've captured, dressed with your school's remarks, then published and printed. Everything lives under **Exams & Grading → Reports & Academic Publishing**.

## 1. Generate reports

1. Open **Exams & Grading → Reports & Academic Publishing → Report Cards**.
2. Use **Report Templates** in the same group first if you want a custom layout.
3. Click **Generate Reports**, choose the **academic year**, **term** and **class stream(s)**, and the template to use. The system builds one report per student from their captured marks.

## 2. Add remarks

- **Class Teacher Remarks** and **Headmaster Remarks** are recorded per report. Use **Add Class Teacher Remarks** to apply one observation across a whole class stream.
- **Per-subject remarks** attach a comment to each subject line.
- **Outstanding Achievements** and competency ratings can be recorded per student where your school uses them.

## 3. Publish

Reports start as **Draft**. Review the marks and remarks, then click **Publish to Portal** on each row — or select several and publish in bulk. Published reports are what families see and what printing uses.

Need to fix something later? **Unpublish from Portal** reopens a report for editing.

## 4. Print

- **Print Report** on a single row prints one student's card.
- **Bulk Print Reports** prints everything currently matching your table filters — as a single combined PDF (best for a whole class) or as a ZIP of individual PDFs.
- **Print Selected** prints just the ticked rows.

> Always spot-check one report before a bulk print — a wrong term setting is much cheaper to catch on one sheet than on two hundred.

Next: **Publish to Student Portal** in the same group controls what families see in their portal.
