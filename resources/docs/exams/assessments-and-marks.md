# Set up assessments and capture marks

Everything graded in the school — class tests, assignments, mid-terms, finals — flows through **Exams & Grading**.

## 1. Create an assessment

1. Open **Exams & Grading → Assessment Center → Digital Assessments**.
2. Click **Create** and give the assessment a **title** (for example "Form 2 Mid-Term Maths").
3. Choose the **subject**, **class stream**, **academic year**, **term** and **assessment type**.
4. Set the **total marks** and **pass mark**, and choose whether it **contributes to the grade**.
5. Optionally add instructions, a duration, attempt limits and question randomisation.
6. Save.

## 2. Capture marks

1. Open **Exams & Grading → Grading & Marks Management → Marks Entry**.
2. Enter marks directly in the table, or use the **populate** action to load every learner in a class and subject at once.
3. Save as you go — marks stay editable until the report they feed is published.

Digital assessments created through the platform can also feed marks in without retyping.

## 3. Review before publishing

Use **Exams & Grading → Grading & Marks Management → Performance Analytics** to check class averages and outliers before you lock anything in. **Grading Scales** in the same group define how marks map to grades and comments.

## 4. From marks to report cards

Once marks are in, generate academic reports — see [Produce report cards](/docs/exams/report-cards).
