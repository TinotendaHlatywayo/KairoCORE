# Set up assessments and capture marks

Everything graded in the school — class tests, assignments, mid-terms, finals — flows through **Exams & Grading**.

## 1. Create an assessment

1. Open **Exams & Grading → Add New Test / Assessment**.
2. Give it a name (for example "Form 2 Mid-Term Maths"), choose the subject, form / grade, term, and the date.
3. Set the total marks or weighting your school uses.
4. Save.

## 2. Capture marks

With the assessment created, open the marks area (**Assessment Marks** in the same section) and enter scores per student — either student by student or through bulk entry for a whole class.

Digital assessments created through the platform can also feed marks in without retyping.

## 3. Review before publishing

Marks remain editable until the results they feed are published. Use the analytics page under **Exams & Grading** to check class averages and outliers before you lock anything in.

## 4. From marks to report cards

Once marks are in, generate academic reports — see [Produce report cards](/docs/exams/report-cards).
