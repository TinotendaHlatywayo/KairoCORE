# Track student fee balances

Every invoice carries a live **balance** — the amount still due after all recorded payments.

## On the invoices table

Open **Finance → Student Billing & Revenue → Invoices**. Each row shows the invoice total and the **Balance** (highlighted in red when money is still owed). Filter the table to find everyone with an outstanding balance, or search for one family.

## On the financial dashboard

Open **Finance → Dashboard & Analytics → Overview** for the school-wide picture: totals billed, collected and outstanding across the current filters, with charts for trends. **Financial Statements** in the same group gives the ledger view.

## For one family

1. Open the student's invoice.
2. Click **Print Statement** for a complete payment history — invoices issued, payments received, and the closing balance.

This is the document to hand a parent, sponsor or agent who asks "what do we still owe?"

## Dealing with arrears

- Use the invoice list filtered to unpaid invoices to run a collection week.
- Statements give families a clear, official record — much more effective than a verbal reminder.
- If a family genuinely cannot pay, apply a **Fee Waiver** or use **Adjust Fee** on the invoice; never delete payment history.
