# Record and categorise school expenses

The Finance section tracks every cost your school incurs, separately from student fees.

## Where expenses live

Open **Finance → Expenses & Purchasing → Expenses**. This is the recorded-expenses list; the **Expenses & Purchasing** hub also links you to the purchasing workflow.

## Categories and types

Expense **categories** are broad buckets (Administration, Utilities, Salaries, Maintenance); **types** are the specific line items inside those buckets (Electricity, Water, Internet, Teacher salaries).

You don't need a separate setup screen — create them while recording an expense:

1. Start a new expense.
2. On the **Expense Category** field, use the inline **create** option and name the bucket.
3. The **Expense Type** field is optional and works the same way.

Categories and types created here become available on every future expense, so good naming makes your reports genuinely useful.

## Record an expense

1. Open **Finance → Expenses & Purchasing → Expenses** and click **Create**.
2. Capture the expense name, category, optional type, amount, bank account, date and reference number.
3. Add supplier/vendor details if the expense was paid to a company.
4. Save.

The expense is now included in financial totals and reports.

## Review

The expenses table can be filtered by category, type, bank account and date range — for example "all utilities in Q3" or "salaries for March". **Finance → Dashboard & Analytics → Overview** folds expenses into the overall financial picture alongside fee collection.

## Purchasing and stock

For purchases of goods and inventory (supplies, library books, lab equipment), use the **Inventory & Procurement** section instead — see [Purchasing, suppliers and stock](/docs/finance/purchasing).
