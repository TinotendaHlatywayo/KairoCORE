# Record and categorise school expenses

The Finance section tracks every cost your school incurs, separately from student fees.

## Set up categories and types first

1. Open **Finance → Expense Categories** — broad buckets (Administration, Utilities, Salaries, Maintenance).
2. Open **Finance → Expense Types** — the specific line items inside those buckets (Electricity, Water, Internet, Teacher salaries).

Good naming here makes your expense reports genuinely useful.

## Record an expense

1. Open **Finance → Recorded Expenses**.
2. Create a new entry: date, category, type, amount, description, and payment method.
3. Save.

The expense is now included in financial totals and reports.

## Review

The recorded-expenses table can be filtered by category, type and date range — for example "all utilities in Q3" or "salaries for March". **Finance → Dashboard & Analytics** folds expenses into the overall financial picture alongside fee collection.

## Purchasing and stock

For purchases of goods and inventory (supplies, library books, lab equipment), use the **Inventory & Procurement** section instead — see [Purchasing and suppliers](/docs/finance/purchasing).
