# Create fee structures and issue invoices

Fee billing in the workspace has two parts: **fee structures** (what each form or grade owes) and **student invoices** (the actual bill per student). Both live under **Finance → Student Billing & Revenue**.

## 1. Fee structures

1. Open **Finance → Student Billing & Revenue → Fee Structures**.
2. Create a structure: choose the academic year, the form / grade it applies to, and the amount.
3. Add fee categories (tuition, levies, activities) if you itemise charges — **Fee Categories** is in the same group.

Structures drive the auto-billing engine and the invoice form's fee picker.

## 2. Issue an invoice manually

1. Open **Finance → Student Billing & Revenue → Invoices**.
2. Click **Create**.
3. Pick the **student**, **academic year** and **term**. The form filters the available fee structures to those matching the student's year and the selected year.
4. Select the fee structures to include. The totals calculate automatically; a fee waiver (if configured) applies as a discount.
5. Save. The invoice is created with status **Unpaid** and a unique invoice number.

## 3. Run the auto-billing engine

For a whole cohort at once:

1. On the invoices table, click **Run Auto-Billing Engine**.
2. Choose the billing target (which forms / grades and term).
3. The engine creates invoices for every eligible student who doesn't already have an open invoice for that scope.

This is the fastest way to bill a year group on the first day of term.

## 4. Documents

Each invoice can be printed (**Print Invoice** on the row). Invoice and receipt layout — logo, colours, payment channels, callout notes — is controlled under **Finance → Core Accounting & Setup → Document Templates**.

## 5. Take payments

Record cash, transfers and mobile money against the invoice, and print receipts — see [Record payments](/docs/finance/recording-payments). Families can also pay through **Pay via Paynow** and submit **Payment Proofs** for your approval.
