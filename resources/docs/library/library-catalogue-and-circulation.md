# Run the library catalogue and circulation

The **Library** module covers the catalogue, electronic resources and day-to-day issuing and returns.

## Catalogue

### Books

Open **Library → Catalogue → Books**. A book record holds:

- **Title**, **subtitle** and **authors** (add author biographies inline where useful)
- **ISBN**, **publisher** and **publication year**
- **Media type** and **library format**
- **Category** (create categories inline)
- **Cover image**
- **Copies** — add a number of physical copies when creating the book

Each copy is a separate item with its own status, so two copies of the same title can be out to different readers. Keep the **copies** section current: a lost copy should be marked, not silently deleted.

### eResources

Open **Library → Catalogue → eResources** for digital material. Each e-resource has a title, media type, format, category, authors and a **view link**. You control whether readers can **view** the resource, **download** it, or both, and whether bulk download is allowed.

## Circulation

### Issue a book

Open **Library → Circulation → Issue Book**. The page walks you through:

1. **Select a book** (search by title or ISBN).
2. **Select a copy** — only copies marked *available* can be issued.
3. **Select a recipient** — a student or a staff member.
4. Set the **return date** (defaults to 14 days).

Confirm, and the copy status changes to *issued*. The reader sees the loan in their portal.

### Issues and returns

Open **Library → Circulation → Issues** to see every loan with its book, copy, recipient, issue date, due date and status. Use it to:

- find **overdue** loans — filter by due date
- **return** a copy when the book comes back (the copy becomes available again)
- check a reader's borrowing history

> Scan the copy's barcode at the desk when issuing and returning — it is faster than searching and avoids issuing the wrong copy.

## Knowledge

The **Knowledge** group stores your school's own reference material:

- **Knowledge Assets** — documents, policies, past papers and guides
- **Galleries** — image collections for events, campus and marketing

These are searchable by staff and can be shared with the school community depending on their settings.
