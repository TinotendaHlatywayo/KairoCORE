# Brag Plan: KairoCORE

## Project understanding
- Product: KairoCORE, a cloud-based school operating system.
- Core message: Run your whole school from one platform.
- Audience: school owners, principals, administrators, bursars/finance, academic administrators, school management teams.
- Strongest real product moments: workspace dashboard, admissions/applications, academic reports, finance accounts, communication threads.
- Brand: white ground, navy #1F2E43 text, coral #EF5F4D / #D94B39 accent, Plus Jakarta Sans display, Inter body.

## Creative angle
Show KairoCORE as the connected operating layer for a school: one workspace where admissions, academics, finance, and communication stop living in separate tools.

## Hook
"One school. Many moving parts."

## Outro / punchline
"Run your whole school from one platform."
